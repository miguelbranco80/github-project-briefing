## The first engineering change

Explain in connected paragraphs what happened before, how the change works, its design trade-offs and the practical consequence. Name concrete components or show a small example when supported by inspected sources. Link primary evidence inline and state naturally whether the behavior is proposed, merged or deployment-verified. Connect related work rather than listing PR summaries.

Add other sections only when they deepen the explanation. Choose specific technical headings and update topics to match. Do not manufacture smaller updates, a conclusion, a diagram or a next-step section to fill the template. Reread the finished draft for understanding, source support and unnecessary prose before publishing.
