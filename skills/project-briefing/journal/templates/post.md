Open with the consequential change and its practical context. State only what the reviewed sources support.

## The first engineering change

Explain the previous limitation, the mechanism that changes it, and its consequence. Link primary sources inline. Say naturally whether this is proposed, a merged design, merged code or author-reported deployment evidence.

## The next connected change

Explain what changed since the last poll. Connect to the earlier post where that helps; do not repeat unchanged background.

## Elsewhere in the update

Combine smaller changes in a short narrative. Keep the full change ledger and tracker underneath.

## What to watch next

Name the next decision or concrete evidence that would change the assessment. Separate interpretation from declared maintainer intent.

<small>Sources and limits: identify the observation cutoff and evidence base. A rewrite of a saved poll is not a fresh check. A test reported by the author is not an independent qualification.</small>
