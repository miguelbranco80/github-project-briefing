# About this journal

This independent journal follows <a href="{{ site.repository }}">{{ site.repositoryName }}</a>: the problems being worked on, the decisions behind them, and how the implementation works.

## What an entry tells you

Each article explains a meaningful development using linked code, proposals and discussions. It connects the previous behavior, the mechanism and the practical consequence. Related work appears together; continuing stories link to earlier entries.

Sources establish whether work is proposed, accepted, merged or verified in deployment. Author-reported tests and our interpretations are identified as such. Each entry states its observation window and material coverage gaps.

## Following the history

Meaningful developments produce new dated articles. Quiet checks leave the archive unchanged. Earlier entries retain their observation dates, and corrections are dated explicitly, so readers can follow how the work and our understanding evolved.
